BattleBlocks AI

You build. AI chooses what falls.

A native Android asymmetric falling-block duel, demonstrated on a physical Solana Seeker.

The twist

One game.
Two different powers.

Builder

Places, rotates and drops pieces against objectives and a clock.

Chooser

Selects the next piece from a constrained visible hand.

Playable proof

Native Android. Physical Seeker.

  • Portrait touch controls and visible game state
  • Offline, wallet-free practice loop
  • Development-only on-device AI Chooser
  • Bounded launch, play, lifecycle, soak and relaunch checks

Debug-signed evidence build; not store/release signed.

Why it matters

The next piece becomes the opponent.

The asymmetry creates readable tension: adapt to a difficult selection before the objective and timer beat you. The hand, next piece and sent-or-used state stay visible.

Why Solana

A trust layer around fast local play.

Planned opt-in publishing can bind an AI version, rules and signed match/replay receipts to portable reputation—without putting real-time gameplay or private history on-chain.

Wallet authorization, signatures, transactions and receipts are planned, not demonstrated in this build.

Roadmap

From one AI role to an AI battle platform.

Human Builder vs AI Chooser
Human Chooser vs AI Builder
Player-trained Builder and Chooser AIs
Opt-in AI publishing and AI-vs-AI challenges
Verifiable battle and reputation points
Future rewards only after legal/platform review
Safety boundary

Useful free play stays independent.

  • Gameplay history local/private by default
  • Publishing requires explicit opt-in
  • No paid competitive strength
  • No wagering or token promise
  • Unverified roadmap is labelled as roadmap
