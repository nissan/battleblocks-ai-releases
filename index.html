The twist

One game.
Two different powers.

Builder

Places, rotates and drops pieces against objectives and a clock.

Chooser

Selects the next piece from a constrained visible hand.

More than endless Tetris

Climb. Recover. Hold.

In Climb, the Builder has 60 active seconds to raise at least three columns to row 8. Activation speeds up the music and starts Recover: clear every column below row 8 within two minutes, then hold for 20 seconds. A missed deadline, overflow or blocked spawn gives the round to the Chooser.

The visible hand and split authority make the next piece an opponent—not just luck.

Why Solana

A trust layer around fast local play.

Planned opt-in publishing can bind an AI version, rules and signed match/replay receipts to portable reputation—without putting real-time gameplay or private history on-chain.

Wallet authorization, signatures, transactions and receipts are planned, not demonstrated in this build.
